# Naming and Identity History

Genesis is the temporary internal codename for the RASL research initiative. No permanent protocol, network, token, or ecosystem name has been selected.

Names explored in prior brainstorming include: AETHER, NORAVA, NEROVA, ARELIS, ORINA, ROTERA, CALAND, ROVEN, MAERA, RIVAND, BAKEN, and MAERO. These were rejected or deferred as candidates because of potential conflicts, existing usage, branding concerns, or suitability questions. The available record does not map a specific reason to each name and does not establish trademark clearance, domain availability, or legal availability for any name.

The eventual name should be internationally approachable, memorable, easy to spell and pronounce, and suitable for a globally relevant foundational technology. Rotterdam’s engineering, maritime, and infrastructure heritage may provide subtle inspiration. A future naming process should include linguistic, cultural, trademark, domain, and existing-use checks before selection.
