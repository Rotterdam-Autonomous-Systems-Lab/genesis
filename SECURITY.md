# Security Policy

Genesis is an early-stage research repository, not a production network. Do not submit real credentials, private keys, seed phrases, wallet files, or personal data in issues, pull requests, or research artifacts. Experiments must use synthetic identities and must not involve real funds or production deployments.

For a potential vulnerability that could affect contributors, users, or a future prototype, avoid public disclosure until maintainers can assess it. Contact the RASL maintainers through the organization’s published security contact, if available. Include affected components, impact, reproduction steps, and a safe mitigation where possible. Do not include secrets in the report.

No response-time or remediation guarantee is made while the project is experimental. Do not test against systems or services without authorization.
