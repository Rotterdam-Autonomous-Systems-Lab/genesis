# Contributing to Genesis

Contributions should advance a research question or improve the clarity, safety, or reproducibility of the work. Start with `MASTER_PLAN.md` and open an issue or discussion describing the question, evidence, and intended deliverable before proposing substantial changes.

Useful contributions include literature reviews with citations, threat-model reviews, formal definitions, reproducible evaluation plans, small simulations after scope review, and editorial improvements. Do not present a hypothesis as a result or claim novelty without prior-art evidence.

Keep changes focused. Explain assumptions and limitations, include sources for factual claims, and document reproducibility details for experiments. Changes to mission, protocol direction, or other major architecture require explicit review and an ADR. Never include secrets, real wallet material, or production credentials. Security concerns should follow `SECURITY.md` rather than being filed publicly.

By contributing, you agree that your work may be distributed under the license eventually selected for the repository; until a license is selected, no project license is granted by this document. See `LICENSE-PROPOSAL.md`.
