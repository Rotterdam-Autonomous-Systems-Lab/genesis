# ADR 0003: Independent L1 and Human/Machine Economy Research Direction

- **Date:** 2026-10-18.
- **Status:** Accepted for research scope only, based on the founder's explicit Task 006 instruction.
- **Authority:** Task 006, “Vision Alignment and Experiment 002 Design Review”; not an inference from experimental results.

## Context and decision

Earlier canonical documents treated an independent network and native asset as optional possibilities. The founder now explicitly identifies an independent, permissionless Layer 1 blockchain economy as the long-term architectural research goal. Investigate AI mining or native-token earning through independently verifiable contributions and meaningful human use of the same economic network/asset, including human-to-human, human-to-machine and machine-to-machine exchange.

Organize planning around **Verifiable Autonomous Work**, **Independent Network Security**, and **Human and Machine Economy**. Retain Autonomous Economic Authority, Verifiable Coordination and Autonomous Network Security as cross-cutting technical tracks; do not erase their history. Seek adaptability across AI, computing, robotics, cryptography and communications without promising complete future-proofing.

## Alternatives and rationale

Keeping the target merely optional would fail to preserve the clarified founder intent. Conversely, selecting a consensus algorithm, supply, ticker, issuance schedule or launch would mistake research direction for a validated design. Record the explicit goal and keep existing networks/assets and non-chain systems as comparison baselines capable of challenging its justification.

## Boundaries, risks and reversibility

No concrete chain architecture, native asset implementation, mining/reward mechanism, economic parameters, deployment, production launch or sustainable outcome is approved. Useful-work validity does not automatically provide scarcity, Sybil resistance, consensus security or demand. Reward farming, collusion, verification cost, concentration and absent human utility could invalidate proposed mechanisms.

The research goal is subject to evidence and explicit direction review; preserve negative results rather than silently narrowing the mission or claiming inevitable success. Historical reconstructions and Experiment 001's published claims remain unchanged. This is a reversible research-scope clarification, not an irreversible architectural/economic decision.
