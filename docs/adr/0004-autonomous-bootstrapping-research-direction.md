# ADR 0004: Autonomous Bootstrapping Research Direction

- **Date:** 2026-10-18.
- **Status:** Accepted for research scope only, per the founder's explicit Task 008 instruction.
- **Scope:** vision preservation and bounded research planning; no implementation, token or deployment authorization.

## Decision and rationale

Preserve Genesis's goal of an independent permissionless **AI-first blockchain economy**, with autonomous agents eventually discovering the network, assessing economic opportunities, acting within bounded cryptographic authority, contributing verifiable work and earning/spending native tokens. Humans and machines ultimately share the same open infrastructure. AI-first identifies research/interface priorities; it confers no consensus privilege or exclusion of human participants.

Genuine observable activity is a potential route to organic human discovery/adoption without traditional marketing as the primary mechanism. The founder explicitly asks to retain this direction; neither existing payment demonstrations nor this decision prove it feasible. Keep it alongside the three pillars and [ADR 0003](0003-independent-l1-research-direction.md).

## Alternatives and boundaries

Reducing Genesis to a payment application or requiring marketing-led adoption would omit the stated ambition. Claiming that present AI will discover Genesis, that a native token exists or is valuable, or that transactions guarantee adoption would invent results. Do not manufacture wallet activity, artificial trading, circular demand or misleading economic signals. Clearly label synthetic tests; public addresses and volume do not establish independent customers.

No supply, issuance schedule, consensus, native asset, protocol architecture, autonomous public deployment or outreach campaign is selected. Discovery identity, initial fee resources, independent useful work, Sybil resistance, funding provenance, privacy and human utility remain open. This reversible research direction may be challenged by evidence; preserve negative outcomes rather than fabricate traction. Adaptability is a research aim, not guaranteed future-proofing.

## Follow-up

The five [autonomous bootstrapping scopes](../../research/AUTONOMOUS_BOOTSTRAPPING_RESEARCH.md) cover safe onboarding, external funding/issuance, useful/duplicate work, fixed-resource Sybils and genuine human discovery. They remain unimplemented. [Experiment 002 blocker resolution](../experiments/EXPERIMENT_002_BLOCKER_RESOLUTION.md) is a separate local technical decision; accepting its design is not evidence of autonomous network adoption.
