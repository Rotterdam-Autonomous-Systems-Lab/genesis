# Genesis Vision

Genesis is the temporary codename for an open research initiative from Rotterdam Autonomous Systems Lab (RASL). Its long-term architectural research goal is an independent, permissionless, AI-first Layer 1 blockchain economy with a native asset for meaningful human and machine economic activity. Task 006 clarifies this goal; [ADR 0003](adr/0003-independent-l1-research-direction.md) records its research-only boundary.

Potential participants include humans and human-operated systems, AI agents and autonomous software, robots and industrial machines, vehicles and drones, satellites, sensors, connected devices, distributed computational systems, and future entities not yet invented. Participants may differ in ownership, hardware, capabilities, jurisdictions, objectives, and connectivity.

The central research question is whether autonomous participants that have never met can establish verifiable agreements, exchange resources, and coordinate safely without trusting each other or a central platform. The guiding principle is: **the network must not need to trust the intelligence of its participants; it must only need to verify their actions.** This is a research principle, not a proven universal guarantee. Some real-world claims may depend on hardware, sensors, operators, external data, or governance.

Genesis is not limited to an AI-agent payment application. Experiments 001 and 002 are bounded investigations of verifiable authority and service payment within this much larger goal. Research should investigate AI mining or native-token rewards through independently verifiable contributions, including useful work, verification cost, consensus and Sybil resistance. Neither AI intelligence nor a count of agent identities establishes contribution or decentralized security.

Humans should have genuine reasons to use the same network and native asset: human-to-human, human-to-machine and machine-to-machine activity all belong in scope. Repeated useful exchange, affordability and sustainable fees/incentives/issuance need evidence; token speculation, reward farming or autonomous transaction volume is insufficient.

The connected research pillars are **Verifiable Autonomous Work**, **Independent Network Security**, and **Human and Machine Economy**. The original authority, coordination and network-security tracks remain technical methods across these pillars. Existing networks, assets and non-chain systems are comparison baselines; evidence may challenge the goal but must not silently redefine it.

The aim is long-term adaptability to developments in AI, computing, robotics, cryptography and communications, with open interfaces, verifiable authority, decentralized security and open participation. Compatibility, migrations and upgrade power need adversarial review. Complete future-proofing is not a guarantee.

The project is in early-stage experimental research. It has not invented or validated a new consensus protocol or cryptographic primitive. No final architecture, token, ticker, consensus algorithm, or permanent network name has been selected. Offline, satellite, and space-based operation are future research contexts, not present capabilities. The ambition to investigate infrastructure with significance to future machine economies is aspirational and does not claim an achievement comparable to Bitcoin or technical novelty.

## Autonomous bootstrapping aspiration — Task 008

Autonomous agents should eventually discover the network, assess opportunities, onboard under bounded cryptographic authority, contribute verifiable work and earn/spend native tokens. AI-first prioritizes autonomous software interfaces while humans and all other machines retain access to the same open infrastructure. [ADR 0004](adr/0004-autonomous-bootstrapping-research-direction.md) records this founder-defined direction.

Genuine observable on-chain activity could let humans discover useful services and voluntarily adopt the network organically, without traditional marketing as the primary mechanism. Current agents are not shown to discover Genesis; no native asset or monetary value exists here; neither activity nor adoption is guaranteed. Initial resources, authenticity, genuine demand, privacy and funding provenance remain open.

Never manufacture wallet activity, artificial trading, circular demand or misleading economic signals. Synthetic local tests remain labelled laboratory evidence, not organic activity. See the five [research scopes](../research/AUTONOMOUS_BOOTSTRAPPING_RESEARCH.md); none is implemented or authorized for public deployment.
