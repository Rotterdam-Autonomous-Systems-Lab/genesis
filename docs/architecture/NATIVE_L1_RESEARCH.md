## Requirements extracted from evidence

“Demonstrated” means finite tests under a trusted local host/RPC, not formally proven or validated permissionless operation. Sources are [Experiment 001 sections 12–15](../../research/reports/EXPERIMENT_001_REPORT.md) and [Experiment 002 final review](../experiments/EXPERIMENT_002_FINAL_REVIEW.md).

| ID | Actual evidence | Native requirement or open extension |
| --- | --- | --- |
| N-01 | Signed immutable terms, domain/run binding, one-use authority and exact price caps reject tested misuse. | Specify canonical encoding, network/genesis identity, versioning, replay scope and atomic authority consumption; no privileged AI identity. |
| N-02 | Correct public arithmetic can settle without a second requester signature in Experiment 002; Experiment 001 intentionally retains that veto. | Represent explicit contingent consent and deterministic validity separately from subjective satisfaction. Preserve both policies as controls, not universal requirements to remove consent. |
| N-03 | Exclusive claim/refund, per-owner credit, separate withdrawal and exact accounting hold in tested paths. | Enforce conservation and recipient ownership across every transition, including fees, reverts and concurrent activity. Credit is not collection. |
| N-04 | Revocation affects unused authority; funding survives later revocation. Deadline validity depends on inclusion, not send time. | Define conflict ordering and time/finality semantics. No retroactive cancellation or silent deadline extension. |
| N-05 | Fee reservation/attempt limits are honest-signer policy, distinct from contract principal limits. | Research ledger-enforced delegated principal and fee ceilings; atomic decrement across parallel delegates. Current experiments did not demonstrate general delegation. |
| N-06 | Contradictory receipts/records and unknown delivery stop signing; rollback can make a run inconclusive. | Authenticated state/inclusion/finality evidence and durable intent recovery are needed. Matching RPC replies are not consensus proof. |
| N-07 | Late inclusion, disclosure, no actor and lost keys can prevent collection. | Explicit abstention/recovery and connectivity envelopes; no unconditional fairness or automatic refund claim. |
| N-08 | Bounded codecs, verification and cleanup work cooperatively on one host. | Bound parsing, signature checks, execution, storage and network admission independently; assess constrained client roles. No native validator hardware budget exists. |

Permissionless security, useful-work scarcity, confidential delivery, offline double-spend prevention, physical truth, secure key recovery, autonomous discovery and real demand remain **aspirations or open hypotheses**. Arithmetic validity establishes none of them. No experiment proves that a new L1 is better than existing-chain or non-chain settlement.

## State and execution choices

State representation, execution language, ordering/finality, scarce-resource admission and client recovery are separate axes. Familiar EVM tooling is an experimental convenience, not evidence that EVM is optimal.

UTXO explicitly consumes prior outputs and creates successors; disjoint inputs offer identifiable independence, but shared funds can contend and wallet input selection becomes part of correctness. Extended UTXO supports richer validation and state-machine constraints; that does not prove a particular Genesis agreement maps safely. Account state naturally represents balances/nonces and shared contracts but makes sequencing and shared-state interference explicit design concerns. Object models expose ownership/version dependencies; owned and shared paths differ, and shared-object access still requires authorization. None makes conflicting writes safely parallel by naming them differently. [Bitcoin §§2,9](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf), [EUTXO paper §§2–4](https://www.iog.io/api/research/pdf/T24L95MI), [Ethereum accounts](https://ethereum.org/developers/docs/accounts/), [Sui ownership](https://www.docs.sui.io/develop/objects/object-ownership).

| Execution alternative | Reason to compare | Required evidence / trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Narrow native transition modules | Small explicit authority/settlement surface; easy reference semantics. | Each added feature becomes protocol maintenance/upgrade work; cannot infer adequate general utility from addition. |
| EVM-style metered bytecode | Existing escrow semantics and developer tools provide a control. | Call/reentrancy, state access and fee-metering complexity; compatibility is an additional constraint, not security inheritance. [EVM specification overview](https://ethereum.org/developers/docs/evm/). |
| Move/resource bytecode | Resource typing prevents certain copying/discarding errors. | Does not prove authorization, pricing or contract invariants; runtime/module upgrades still require review. Move is not synonymous with an object ledger. [Original Move paper §§3–6](https://developers.diem.com/papers/diem-move-a-language-with-programmable-resources/2020-04-09.pdf). |
| Restricted Wasm | Portable execution format with configurable embedding. | Pin deterministic numeric profile/NaN treatment, permitted host calls, instruction/memory/storage metering and runtime conformance. Generic Wasm alone is not a blockchain execution specification. [Numerics](https://webassembly.github.io/spec/core/exec/numerics.html), [embedding](https://webassembly.github.io/spec/core/appendix/embedding.html). |
| Off-chain execution with validity proofs or optimistic disputes | Potentially avoid replicating expensive work. | Bind program/input/output and availability; quantify prover/verifier costs or honest-watcher/challenge-window assumptions. Defer as initial default; [prior comparison](../../research/experiments/EXPERIMENT_002_FAIR_EXCHANGE_COMPARISON.md) supplies research context, not Genesis proof-system validation. |

Payment channels and HTLCs are additional settlement controls: funding, monitoring/recovery and timing assumptions remain, and revealing a preimage does not prove service correctness. They can reduce repeated base-layer interactions but do not replace consensus; compare them separately using the [existing fair-exchange review](../../research/experiments/EXPERIMENT_002_FAIR_EXCHANGE_COMPARISON.md).

## Consensus and Sybil resistance

The following are established families with different assumptions, not interchangeable product guarantees. Maturity assessments are qualitative inference from the cited specifications and deployed reference families; no release audit or comparative benchmark was reproduced here.

| Family | Security premise and finality | Cost, attacks and maturity boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nakamoto hash PoW | Weight follows verifiable hash work, not identities; probabilistic settlement under honest-work/network assumptions. | Recurrent energy/hardware, pooling, hash rental/concentration, selfish mining, eclipse and reorg exposure. Bitcoin is an established reference; a small new chain does not inherit its hash security. “Below 50%” is not a universal safety theorem independent of propagation. [Whitepaper §§4,11](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf), [Backbone analysis](https://eprint.iacr.org/2014/765), [selfish-mining model](https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.0243v5). |
| Stake-weighted BFT | For a CometBFT-style active set, safety assumes less than one-third Byzantine voting power; commits need more than two-thirds; progress needs sufficient responsive power and eventual communication bounds. | Capital, online signing, bandwidth/storage; censorship, correlated outages, equivocation and stake concentration. Membership/rotation, unbonding and admission are additional protocol obligations. CometBFT specifies ordering, not a permissionless stake economy. [Pinned consensus spec](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cometbft/cometbft/v0.38.17/spec/consensus/consensus.md). |
| PoS fork choice plus finality gadget | Ethereum illustrates separate head selection/finality and bonded penalties; long-offline sync requires a trusted recent checkpoint. | Lower ongoing hash expenditure does not remove capital/availability costs or long-range, concentration and recovery risks. Its running system is evidence for that design, not Genesis parameters. [PoS](https://ethereum.org/developers/docs/consensus-mechanisms/pos/), [weak subjectivity](https://ethereum.org/developers/docs/consensus-mechanisms/pos/weak-subjectivity/). |
| Storage-weighted consensus | Filecoin specifies proofs/storage power, leader selection and randomness, not generic AI-quality voting. | Specialized proving/storage, collateral and beacon dependencies; domain-specific scarcity and incentive review needed. Relevant research comparator, deferred from first candidates. [Storage Power Consensus](https://spec.filecoin.io/systems/filecoin_blockchain/storage_power_consensus/). |

HotStuff is another partially synchronous BFT ordering reference, not a new Sybil resource or a reason to copy its benchmark to Genesis. Static authority/identity committees can be laboratory controls but fail the open participation goal unless a separate permissionless membership mechanism is established. AI reputations or “one agent, one vote” supply no scarce resource. Keep useful-work validity, paid service demand, reward eligibility and consensus weight separate. [HotStuff abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.05069v6), [Douceur, The Sybil Attack](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-sybil-attack/).

## Machine participation and native-currency hypotheses

Devices need not all be validators. Distinguish full validators, independently verifying full nodes, light clients and relayed clients; list each one's data/checkpoint/relay trust. A robot's physical safety controller must not wait for an unbounded ledger decision. Satellite/intermittent clients may queue signed intent and reconcile later; they cannot assume fresh revocation state or prevent offline double spending without additional bounded risk/trust. A partition may require halting economic action even while local computation continues.

Research grants bound purpose, recipient, principal, fee, expiry, resource use and aggregate concurrent spending. Restrict subdelegation; define revocation races and preserve funded obligations. Ledger-enforced grants and locally isolated/hardware signers address different threats. Recovery needs a durable public intent identifier, idempotent reconciliation, authenticated finality and an explicit key-rotation/recovery authority; no silent fresh-nonce retry. Existing Cosmos SDK `SendAuthorization`/fee allowances are concrete separate-budget prior art, not adopted dependencies; its unlimited empty allowance is an unsafe default for this proposed machine profile. [Authz v0.50.11](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cosmos/cosmos-sdk/v0.50.11/x/authz/README.md), [feegrant v0.50.11](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cosmos/cosmos-sdk/v0.50.11/x/feegrant/README.md).

A native currency could price scarce execution/storage, compensate security providers or bond accountable behavior, and transfer payment for actual services. These roles do not demonstrate necessity, value or sustainable revenue. Separate customer payments from issuance and from security compensation. Fees destroyed are not validator revenue; EIP-1559 is one counterexample to equating the two. [EIP-1559](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-1559).

Following [existing economic research](../../research/economics/AI_EARNED_NATIVE_TOKENS_AND_UTILITY.md), compare low/zero genuine demand, congestion, falling purchasing power, externally subsidized participation and concentrated control. Count independently funded service consumption and total user costs, not synthetic volume or address counts. Model operator compensation and attack incentives in real resource/capital terms; minted units alone cannot guarantee purchasing power or security. Native-fee settlement may coexist with externally denominated quotes, but conversion/oracles/bridges add risks. No supply, distribution, rewards, monetary policy, asset or launch is selected.

## Recommendation and review scope

Retain three **candidate families** for comparative specifications: account/module-or-VM with stake-BFT; owned/shared-object/resource VM with stake-BFT; extended-UTXO/restricted validation with Nakamoto PoW as a contrasting security baseline. The [matrix](NATIVE_L1_DECISION_MATRIX.md) explains trade-offs; pairings are test configurations, not inseparable design rules. EVM remains a control, and existing-network/non-chain alternatives must be capable of defeating the independent-L1 benefit hypothesis.

**Not ready to select a final architecture.** Missing evidence includes workload/device budgets, permissionless bootstrap economics, dynamic-membership/recovery analysis, independent semantics and adversarial resource measurements. First authorize the [comparative specification gate](NATIVE_L1_DECISION_GATE.md); only later separately authorize disposable reference models. No native blockchain is implemented here.
