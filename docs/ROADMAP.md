# Research roadmap

This roadmap lists research gates rather than implementation commitments. Sequence and scope may change through documented review.

1. **Frame the problem:** define actors, use cases, constraints, and success criteria across the three research tracks.
2. **Map prior art:** collect and annotate work on cryptographic authority, capability security, distributed coordination, service verification, Sybil resistance, and protocol evolution.
3. **Review the threat model:** refine assets, adversaries, trust assumptions, and failure cases with community input.
4. **Derive requirements:** state testable security, privacy, availability, and cost requirements; maintain competing hypotheses.
5. **Design evaluations:** select baselines, metrics, and reproducible methods that can disconfirm hypotheses.
6. **Run bounded experiments:** after review, conduct isolated simulations or toy prototypes with synthetic data only.
7. **Publish findings:** report results and limitations, including negative outcomes, and record major decisions in ADRs.

No milestone authorizes a production deployment, token, or consensus implementation. Any such change requires a new explicit project decision.
