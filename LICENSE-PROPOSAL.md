# License proposal

No license has been selected. Until maintainers make and record that decision, do not assume this repository grants permission to reuse its contents.

| License | Summary | Considerations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apache-2.0 | Permissive reuse with an express patent license, notice and attribution requirements, and patent-termination terms. | More explicit patent treatment; longer and more detailed terms. |
| MIT | Short permissive license requiring preservation of copyright and license notices; includes a broad warranty disclaimer. | Simple and widely used; does not contain an express patent license. |

The project should review contributor ownership, patent considerations, dependencies, and community expectations before selecting a license. Record the decision and add the corresponding standard license text only after approval.
